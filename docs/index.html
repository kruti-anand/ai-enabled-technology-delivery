TECHNOLOGY DELIVERY

What I consider across the delivery lifecycle

Effective technology delivery requires more than tracking work. It requires continuous visibility into outcomes, dependencies, risks, decisions, readiness, and alignment from planning through release.

01

PLAN

  • Business outcomes & scope
  • Requirements & assumptions
  • Risks & dependencies
  • Architecture & technical considerations
  • Capacity & sequencing
  • Delivery readiness
  • Governance & decision ownership
02

EXECUTE

  • Progress & predictability
  • Cross-team dependencies
  • Risks, issues & blockers
  • Decisions & escalations
  • Quality & testing readiness
  • Stakeholder alignment
  • Change management
03

DELIVER

  • Release readiness
  • Business / technical / operational readiness
  • Traceability to original intent
  • Outcome measurement
  • Production feedback
  • Lessons learned
  • Continuous improvement

AI ENABLEMENT

Where AI can strengthen delivery

AI can augment delivery practices by reducing manual effort, improving visibility, and helping teams work with more structured information.

01

Structure

Turn ambiguous information into organized delivery artifacts.

02

Surface

Identify gaps, risks, dependencies, assumptions, and unresolved decisions earlier.

03

Synthesize

Reduce manual effort across planning, status, documentation, and governance.

04

Connect

Improve traceability from business intent through solution and execution.

AI assists with analysis and acceleration. Humans remain accountable for validation, prioritization, approvals, and delivery decisions.
